    Demo data. 29 public events for 2027, checked 2 Nov 2026. Confirmed means the organiser or an official source lists the 2027 dates. Projected means the dates come from event aggregators or prior-edition patterns, or sources disagree. Each event links to its source.

    Fiscal year-ends: U.S. federal 30 Sep, UK, Canada and Japan 31 Mar, Australia 30 Jun.

    Method: events are sorted by your weights, with must-go events placed first. Each one goes to the first traveller free across its dates plus travel days, within budget and the quarterly cap. A swap pass then replaces lower-scoring events when one higher-scoring event fits in their place. It's a good plan, not a proven optimum.
